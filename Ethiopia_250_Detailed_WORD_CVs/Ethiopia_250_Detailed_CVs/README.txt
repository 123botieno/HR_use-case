ETHIOPIA 250 DETAILED SYNTHETIC CV DATASET

250 fictional CVs + 5 job descriptions, all in Microsoft Word (.docx).
50 candidates per discipline: 10 Entry, 10 Junior, 10 Intermediate, 10 Senior, 10 Expert.

The seniority levels intentionally differ in depth:
Entry: short education/project-focused profile.
Junior: early work history and practical responsibilities.
Intermediate: multiple roles and measurable improvements.
Senior: longer career progression, leadership, mentoring and portfolio delivery.
Expert: extensive career history, leadership, professional contributions, training and strategic work.

All names, employers, contact details, achievements and candidate histories are fictional.
This dataset is for training/demo/testing and must not be represented as real Ethiopiawork candidate data.
