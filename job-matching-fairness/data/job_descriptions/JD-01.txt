Job Summary / Purpose of the Position

The Senior Data Scientist position supports the organisation’s work in health / data science. The advertisement specifies the education, experience and technical requirements below.

Roles and Responsibilities

Collaborate with public-health teams to convert policy questions into modelling objectives, prediction horizons and validation plans.

Harmonize health, surveillance, climate, survey and geospatial datasets, including boundaries and missing values.

Build Python-based reproducible feature engineering and data preparation workflows.

Develop spatial risk mapping, hotspot and small-area prediction models using leakage-aware spatial validation.

Evaluate CNN, GNN and representation learning where appropriate to available data.

Develop and compare XGBoost, LightGBM, LSTM, GRU and transformer-based forecasting models.

Assess calibration, uncertainty, subgroup and geographic robustness, temporal and spatial leakage, and bias.

Deliver documented workflows, dashboards, maps, APIs, policy briefs and manuscripts.

Mentor analysts and work with epidemiologists, policymakers and technical teams while meeting privacy and security requirements.

Qualifications and Experience

PhD in a quantitative discipline plus 2 years, or Master’s plus 6 years of relevant experience. Six public-health peer-reviewed papers, three first-author.

Required and Desired Skills

Python required; R preferred; SQL; spatial ML; deep learning; time-series forecasting; health data; Git and reproducible analysis.
