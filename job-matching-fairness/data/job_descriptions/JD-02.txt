Job Summary / Purpose of the Position

The Senior Bayesian Spatial Modeller and Small Area Estimation Expert position supports the organisation’s work in health / biostatistics. The advertisement specifies the education, experience and technical requirements below.

Roles and Responsibilities

Frame policy questions by indicator, geography, time period, estimand and decision threshold.

Combine survey, census, routine health, surveillance and geospatial covariates with traceable metadata.

Handle boundary changes, spatial misalignment, displaced survey clusters, missingness and measurement error.

Produce design-based direct estimates and standard errors before model-based estimation.

Fit Fay–Herriot, BYM/BYM2, CAR, Gaussian-process or related Bayesian spatial models as justified.

Use INLA, MCMC, HMC or NUTS and compare model diagnostics, assumptions and sensitivity.

Quantify uncertainty and communicate subnational estimates through maps, reports and policy products.

Train and mentor analysts and support reproducible statistical workflows.

Qualifications and Experience

PhD in statistics, biostatistics, demography, survey methodology or related discipline plus 2 years; or Master’s plus 6 years. Six public-health papers, three first-author.

Required and Desired Skills

Bayesian inference, small area estimation, spatial statistics, R, INLA/Stan, GIS, survey analysis and uncertainty assessment.
