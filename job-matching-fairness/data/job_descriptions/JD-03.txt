The Regional Procurement Officer position supports the organisation’s work in agriculture / procurement. The advertisement specifies the education, experience and technical requirements below.

Roles and Responsibilities

Execute procurement of goods, services and works under ATI, government and donor rules.

Maintain procurement supporting records in ERP and electronic government procurement systems.

Track assigned purchase requisitions and update procurement status daily.

Maintain and periodically update the approved vendor list.

Follow contract execution and assist the Procurement Endorsing Committee with evaluation reports.

Support regional operations leadership with timely and compliant sourcing.

Qualifications and Experience

Bachelor’s in Management, Purchasing/Supply Management, Logistics or related plus 4 years; or Master’s plus 2 years.

Government and donor procurement procedures; ERP and e-GP; procurement reporting; supplier management.
